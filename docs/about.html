About This Project

A simple, fast way to track flights at Islamabad International Airport.

Islamabad Flight Tracker allows users to search and review flight information related to Islamabad International Airport.

You can search by flight number, airline code and date, and filter by arrivals, departures, route type and city. Each flight has a history page showing how its status and times changed. Recent searches are saved on your device only.

It is made for people in Islamabad, travellers coming to Islamabad, and the families, hotels and drivers who pick them up.

All times come from the airports' public flight boards, as published by the Pakistan Airports Authority. They show what each airport reports, not where the aircraft is. For domestic flights we also read the departure airport's board, and say so when it shows something Islamabad's board has not caught up with.

For international arrivals, the day a flight left its origin airport (used for the FlightStats links) comes from AeroDataBox where available.
